About me

I'm Carlos Escribano, a video game developer holding a Bachelor's Degree in Video Game Design and Development and a Master's Degree in Computer Graphics, Games, and Virtual Reality, both from Rey Juan Carlos University in Madrid. I have been creating video games and projects for five years, actively contributing to both narrative design and programming. I have extensive experience with Unity, C++ and graphics APIs, having developed 2D and 3D projects for multiple platforms.

I hope you enjoy my projects!
