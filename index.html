About me

I'm Carlos Escribano, a video game developer holding a degree in Video Game Design and Development and a master's degree in Computer Graphics, Games, and Virtual Reality, both from Rey Juan Carlos University in Madrid. I create video games and projects, ranging from academic work to Game Jam entries, actively contributing to both narrative design and programming. I have extensive experience with Unity, having developed 2D and 3D projects for multiple platforms, including the creation of specialized tools and their commercialization on the Unity Asset Store.

I hope you enjoy my projects!
